## 📋 2. DETAILED SECTION ARCHITECTURE & SPECIFICATIONS

### [01] HERO SECTION
- **Primary Objective:** Hook visitor within 3 seconds, communicate unique value proposition (UVP) and establish tier-one credibility.
- **Mandatory Elements:**
  - Single semantic `<h1>` tag with high-conversion plain-English headline (under 10-12 words).
  - Subheading (2-3 concise lines) explaining the exact technical capability and benefit.
  - Primary CTA button (e.g. `Book 15-Min Architect Session` or `Launch Project`) with subtle glow.
  - Secondary CTA link (e.g. `Explore Portfolio ↓` or `View Squads`).
  - Trust indicators: 15-Minute Architect SLA badge, 99.98% Uptime SLA and Zero-Slop Guarantee.
  - Visual focal point: Real 3D device showcase (MacBook Pro, iPad Pro, or iPhone dynamic island using Aceternity/Magic UI), interactive canvas or high-contrast interface capture. Never flat CSS rectangles.

### [02] LOGO SECTION (SOCIAL PROOF MARQUEE)
- **Primary Objective:** Immediate authority reinforcement through enterprise client, alumni and partner associations.
- **Mandatory Elements:**
  - Heading or eyebrow: `TRUSTED BY FOUNDERS, ENTERPRISES AND ARCHITECTURAL LEADERS WORLDWIDE`.
  - Infinite smooth marquee scroll with hardware-accelerated CSS/canvas (`will-change: transform`).
  - Authentic inline monochrome SVG paths (`fill-zinc-500 hover:fill-white transition-colors`).
  - Left and right gradient edge masks (`from-zinc-950 via-transparent to-zinc-950`) for seamless fading.
  - Zero plain text inside bordered boxes.

### [03] ABOUT US SECTION (FOUNDRY AUTHORITY & POD MODEL)
- **Primary Objective:** Build deep institutional trust, explain operational methodology and eliminate freelance agency skepticism.
- **Mandatory Elements:**
  - Origin & longevity narrative (*Operating since 2019, unified under ZenVQ*).
  - The **3-Person Dedicated Pod** architecture (Lead Architect + Senior Full-Stack Engineer + Dedicated QA/Motion Specialist).
  - 4-column metric counter grid: Verified Projects Delivered, Global Enterprise Clients, Uptime SLA and Average Speed Index (< 500ms).
  - Founder & leadership authority attribution without exposing personal staff lines on agency white-label portals.

### [04] FORM SECTION (INTERACTIVE SCOPING & LEAD FUNNEL)
- **Primary Objective:** Capture high-intent prospect specifications before cognitive fatigue sets in.
- **Mandatory Elements:**
  - Interactive project scoping interface (`LeadFunnelModal.tsx` or inline container).
  - Tiered selection toggles: Project Type (Full-Stack Web, 3D Interactive, Shopify Plus, Custom SaaS), Timeline and Budget Tier.

### [06] INTERACTIVE DEMO / PLAYGROUND SECTION
- **Primary Objective:** Active user engagement through interactive hands-on exploration rather than passive reading.
- **Mandatory Elements:**
  - Interactive multi-device perspective switcher (Desktop, Tablet, Mobile) or live code/feature sandbox.
  - Smooth 3D transforms (`rotateX`, `translateZ`, `scale`) responding to mouse/touch coordinates.
  - Interactive tabs switching between real system states (e.g. Architecture Mode, Performance Mode, Security Shield).
  - Zero mock animations that fail to respond to user interaction.
